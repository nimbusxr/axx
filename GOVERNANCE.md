# Governance

axx is maintained by NimbusXR and its community contributors.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, cut releases, and act as Community Moderators for
  the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Current maintainers are listed in
  [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
- **Contributors** are anyone who opens an issue, discussion or pull request.

During the beta, a lead maintainer has final say on direction. As regular contributors emerge,
they are invited to become maintainers after a sustained record of high-quality reviews and
changes.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes: pull request review by a maintainer.
- Changes to public contracts (Gherkin step text, CLI JSON output and exit codes, `axx.yaml`
  schema): an Architecture Decision Record in `docs/adr/`, discussed in the PR
  or a GitHub Discussion before merging.
